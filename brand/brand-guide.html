Superior Land & Site Services

Driveways, land clearing, grading, ponds and site work across the 937. This guide turns the new logo sketch into a working system for the website, trucks, signs and social.

01 · Who we are

Built for tough ground.

Superior is an owner-run heavy-equipment outfit. They show up with the right machine, move a lot of dirt fast, and leave the site clean: graded, seeded and strawed. The brand should feel like the equipment: heavy, direct, and well kept.

Tagline options (pick one): Built for tough ground. · Clear it. Grade it. Finish it. · Superior work from the ground up.

Heavy-duty

Real machines and real jobs. Show the Deere, the CTL and the gooseneck. No stock photos.

Straight talk

Plain words and short sentences. Say what gets done, how fast, and where.

Clean finish

Every job ends graded, seeded and strawed. Lead with the "after" photo.

02 · Logo

The badge

The sketch sets the look: a chrome-edged chevron badge, a slanted orange SUPERIOR wordmark, a pine-tree mark and a green SERVICES band. Before it goes on the web, vinyl or embroidery it needs a vector redraw:

  • Fix the wordmark. The final "R" is cut off at the right edge.
  • Make a version without the phone number. Numbers change, and at small sizes they turn to noise.
  • Make a flat 2–3 color version with no chrome, glow or gradient for screen printing, favicons and decals.
  • Export SVG, plus PNG at 512 and 1024 px, plus a 1-color white knockout.

Lockups needed

Primary badge

Full chevron with trees and SERVICES band. For the hero, truck doors, yard signs and social avatars.

Horizontal wordmark

SUPERIOR over LAND & SITE, no badge. For the site header (≤ 56px tall), invoices and email signatures.

Icon

Pine trees in a small chevron. For the favicon, app icon and watermark on job photos.

Do

  • Leave clear space at least as tall as the "S" in SUPERIOR on every side.
  • Keep the badge at least 120px wide on screen and 1.5" in print.
  • Place it on white, Blackout, or a darkened photo.

Don't

  • Stretch it, rotate it or add another skew. The lean is already built in.
  • Recolor the wordmark green, or the band orange.
  • Put the full-color badge on a busy photo without a dark scrim.

Seasonal variant: a red, white and blue "250" badge is in the Facebook photos (assets-source/brand/logo-america250-variant.jpg). Save it for 2026 celebration posts only. It is not part of the core identity.

04 · Typography

Type

The logo uses heavy, slanted, condensed capitals. Barlow Condensed ExtraBold Italic is the closest free match for headlines. Its sister family Barlow keeps body copy easy to read. Both are free on Google Fonts, and the site will self-host them.

Rules
  • Headlines: uppercase, 800 italic, line-height 1.0
  • Body: 17–18px, 400, line-height 1.6, max 70 characters per line
  • Buttons: display face, 800 italic, uppercase
  • Keep italics for display text. Don't set body copy in italic.

06 · Photography

Real jobs, real iron

93 job photos were pulled from the Facebook page into assets-source/photos/, each tagged by service in manifest.tsv. Some strong candidates for the hero and service cards:

Shoot & pick

  • Blue-sky, landscape shots for heroes. Portrait shots go in the gallery.
  • Get a machine in frame whenever you can, with the Deere yellow against green grass.
  • Take before and after from the same spot. It's the strongest proof there is.
  • Fly the drone over every pond, lot or commercial job.

Treatment

  • Warm the color a little and don't over-saturate. Keep it looking real.
  • Behind hero text, add a Blackout gradient at 55–70%.
  • Crop out customer plates, house numbers and faces unless you have a release.
  • Rename files by job and town (pond-grading-troy-oh.jpg) for SEO.
