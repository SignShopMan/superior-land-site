# CRO audit: Superior Land & Site Services

- **Site:** https://signshopman.github.io/superior-land-site/ (live build, commit after "Home hero: slow drone-style pan")
- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Method:** `~/.claude/cro-audit-rubric.md`, 12 weighted areas, 5 binary-ish sub-criteria each (2 pts; 1 pt where a criterion is partly met)
- **Pages reviewed:** home, Commercial Site Work, Municipal & ROW, About, Request a Bid (contact), Projects, School Facility project, Service Area, Thanks
- **Form test:** live contact form at 375px; submit intercepted with `preventDefault`, so no lead was sent
- **Speed:** Lighthouse 12 run locally against the live site (the PageSpeed Insights API was over its anonymous daily quota)

The site is built well: clear, fast and easy to act on. It loses most of its points on **credibility**. Trust and Social Proof together are 21% of the weight and score 1/20. A GC or township has no evidence of who Superior is, who runs it, who they've worked for, or whether they're insured.
